Lawn Care Tips & FAQ

A few simple habits can help keep your yard looking its best through the changing seasons.

Seasonal Lawn Care Tips

Spring

Clear leftover leaves and small debris, check lawn areas for winter damage, and plan regular maintenance as grass begins growing.

Summer

Keep a consistent mowing routine and avoid cutting grass excessively short during hot, dry periods.

Fall

Remove fallen leaves when they build up, clear walkways, and prepare outdoor spaces for colder weather.

Winter

Limit unnecessary foot traffic on dormant or frozen grass and keep outdoor areas clear of debris.
